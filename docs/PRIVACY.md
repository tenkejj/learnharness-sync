# Privacy

The native app has no account, telemetry, cloud backend, third-party signaling or mandatory relay. DNS-SD advertises device name, public certificate/fingerprint and local address on the LAN. Choose a name you are comfortable exposing to that network. Pairing requires full session-string comparison and approval on both computers.

Only invented fixtures in purpose-created native profiles can be published. No production LearnHarness content or credentials are read. Private keys and trust stay in the local profile, outside transport; authorized peers receive verified immutable history reachable from the advertised head. Independent local backups are not exposed as a general peer-read API.

Local events describe operations without logging learning content. Profiles and backups are not encrypted at rest. Protect the profile with normal filesystem permissions. Do not include private profile files, invitations, real sessions or unrelated desktop windows in bug reports.
